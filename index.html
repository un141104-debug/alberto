About Alberto Watch Company

Alberto Watch Company is a premier full-service watch repair, appraisal, and luxury retail establishment dedicated to the appreciation of fine timepieces. Founded with a passion for horological mastery, we offer an unparalleled selection of prestigious new watches alongside vintage heirlooms.

In addition to our curated retail collection featuring renowned brands such as Rolex, Michael Kors, Citizen Eco-Drive, and Bulova, our workshop is staffed by certified master watchmakers equipped to handle everything from minor battery replacements to complex mechanical movement overhauls.
